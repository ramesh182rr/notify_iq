# NotifyIQ Brand Notes

- Name: NotifyIQ
- Tagline: Your notifications. Understood.
- Voice: calm, clear, helpful, non-judgemental, privacy-transparent.
- Symbol: notification bell with a subtle intelligence spark.
- Primary palette: indigo/purple; neutral surfaces; restrained semantic accents.
- Avoid: alarmist language, surveillance imagery, excessive gradients, claims of guaranteed importance detection.
- App icon should remain legible at small Android launcher sizes; use the supplied SVG as a starting vector, then export adaptive foreground/background layers.
