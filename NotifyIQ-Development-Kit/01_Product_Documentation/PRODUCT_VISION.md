# Product Vision

## One-line definition
NotifyIQ is a privacy-first Android companion that helps people understand, prioritise, organise, search and act on the notifications they already receive.

## Promise
**Your notifications. Understood.**

## Positioning
NotifyIQ is not a replacement for Android's notification shade. It is a companion intelligence layer with a searchable local history, smart organisation, digest, configurable rules and insights.

## Product pillars
1. Understand — classify notification intent and type.
2. Prioritise — surface likely important alerts with explainable, editable scores.
3. Organise — inbox, category views, grouping, history and saved records.
4. Retrieve — fast search over permitted retained content.
5. Control — user-defined rules, digests, exclusions and supported actions.
6. Learn — show aggregated notification patterns without creating invasive profiling.

## Target audience
General Android users first, with advanced capabilities for high-volume users and productivity enthusiasts.

## Experience principle
The system shade remains the immediate delivery channel. NotifyIQ is where users review, organise, search and reflect. Do not make users open NotifyIQ to receive time-sensitive system alerts.

## Differentiation
- Local-first searchable notification memory.
- Daily briefing that distinguishes observed events from inferred summaries.
- Cross-app grouping by event where confidence is high.
- Transparent privacy controls and exclusions.
- Useful baseline without an account or network connection.

## Out of scope for initial release
- Replacing the Android notification shade.
- Guaranteed interception or suppression of every app notification.
- Reading full message history beyond content exposed in a notification.
- Banking integration or authoritative financial ledger.
- Automated replies or actions without explicit user initiation.
- Cross-device sync of raw notification content by default.
